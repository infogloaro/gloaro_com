## 5. Two distinct partnership models

### A. GLOARO Franchise

For eligible entrepreneurs, business owners, technology professionals,
local business operators, and organizations interested in establishing
and operating an approved GLOARO franchise.

Explain: - What a franchise is and how its operating relationship may
work. - Potential products/services offered, subject to approval. -
Operator responsibilities and company responsibilities. - Local business
development and market considerations. - Eligibility, readiness,
evaluation, due diligence, agreement, and onboarding. - Training,
support, territory, and commercial terms only as confirmed by an
approved agreement. - How to apply.

Visual story: building and operating a technology business with GLOARO.

### B. GLOARO Channel Partner

For IT consultants, software consultants, digital agencies, freelancers,
business consultants, networking professionals, and eligible
organizations interested in introducing customer opportunities to
GLOARO.

Explain: - How to identify and submit a business opportunity. - Which
approved services may be introduced. - Difference between referring a
lead, qualifying an opportunity, managing sales, and completing a
sale. - Partner, GLOARO, and customer responsibilities. - How leads are
reviewed and how progress may be communicated. - Commercial arrangements
only according to the approved agreement. - How to apply.

Visual story: business connections, referrals, collaboration, and
technology opportunities.

**Never merge the models.** Each model must have its own page/journey,
application form, explicit application type, success screen, and backend
record.

## 8. Separate application forms

Both forms must be accessible, multi-step, validated, and connected to a
real backend. A decorative form or frontend-only success message is not
acceptable.

### Franchise application --- five steps

**Step 1: Personal information** - Full name - Email - Phone with
country code - City and state - Applicant type: Individual or Business

**Step 2: Business profile** - Existing business name, if applicable -
Industry - Professional/business experience - Current role - Optional
website

**Step 3: Franchise interest** - Preferred territory/service area -
Products/services of interest - Intended involvement level - Expected
launch timeframe

**Step 4: Readiness** - Business readiness information - Investment
readiness range only if officially approved - Optional business
plan/additional information, if secure upload is supported - Questions
for the GLOARO team

**Step 5: Review and submit** - Show all answers and allow edits -
Required privacy consent linked to the real privacy policy - Separate
optional marketing consent - Submit

### Channel Partner application --- five steps

**Step 1: Contact information** - Full name - Email - Phone with country
code - City and state

**Step 2: Professional profile** - Partner category - Organization name,
if applicable - Optional website/professional profile - Professional
experience - Business network/target customer profile

**Step 3: Partnership interest** - Approved GLOARO services of
interest - Target industries - Expected involvement - Preferred
engagement model only where approved

**Step 4: Additional information** - How the applicant expects to
introduce business opportunities - Questions/additional information -
Optional referral source

**Step 5: Review and submit** - Show all answers and allow edits -
Required privacy consent - Separate optional marketing consent - Submit

### Form behavior

-   Clear progress indicator and "Step N of 5".
-   Required-field indicators and inline validation.
-   Accessible error announcements and labels.
-   Back/Continue actions.
-   Loading state while submitting.
-   Prevent accidental duplicate submissions.
-   Preserve answers after API failures and allow retry.
-   Show success only after actual persistence.
-   Display the server-generated unique reference.
-   Never request passwords, banking credentials, or unnecessary
    sensitive documents.

Validate on both client and server.

## 9. Backend, database, and security

Audit the existing backend/database first and reuse existing
conventions. Do not create duplicate endpoints if equivalent
functionality already exists.

Each record should support: - Server-generated unique reference. -
Explicit application type: `FRANCHISE` or `CHANNEL_PARTNER`. - Contact
information and structured answers. - Consent values and timestamps. -
Created/updated timestamps. - Status. - Assigned reviewer if
supported. - Internal review notes. - Audit history for important
changes.

Suggested statuses, adapted to existing workflow: `SUBMITTED`,
`UNDER_REVIEW`, `SHORTLISTED`, `DISCUSSION`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`.

Possible endpoints, only if equivalent endpoints do not already exist: -
`POST /api/partnership-applications/franchise` -
`POST /api/partnership-applications/channel-partner` -
`GET /api/admin/partnership-applications` -
`GET /api/admin/partnership-applications/:id` -
`PATCH /api/admin/partnership-applications/:id/status`

Security requirements: - Server-side schema validation. - Admin
authentication and role-based authorization. - Rate limiting and spam
protection. - Safe error responses and duplicate-submission
protection. - Protect personally identifiable information. - Never
expose internal notes or other applicants' data publicly. - Audit
important administrative changes. - Secure document handling if uploads
are supported. - Privacy-conscious retention/deletion. - Never send
application personal data to analytics. - Do not log secrets or
unnecessary personal information.

Integrate existing email delivery infrastructure if configured. Notify
the applicant and internal team only through approved, configured flows.
If backend infrastructure is missing, document the exact integration
boundary and do not fake production submission.

## 11. Success/error experiences and conversion analytics

### Success

Show: - Application received. - Application type. - Unique
server-generated reference. - Brief explanation of the next review
stage. - Approved company contact details. - Copy-reference action.

Do not promise acceptance, revenue, commission, or an unconfirmed
callback timeframe.

### Failure

-   Show a useful error.
-   Preserve form values.
-   Allow retry.
-   Prevent duplicate submission.
-   Never show a false success state.

### Analytics

Where allowed by the existing consent/privacy setup, track events such
as: - Franchise CTA clicked. - Channel Partner CTA clicked. -
Partnership comparison viewed. - Application started. - Application step
completed. - Validation error occurred. - Application successfully
submitted.

Do not include names, emails, phone numbers, application answers, or
other personal data in analytics. Do not use manipulative countdowns,
false scarcity, or unsupported social proof.
